About Made to Layer

Made to Layer is a custom 3D printing service created to make bringing digital designs to life simple and accessible. Customers can upload their 3D files, choose their preferred printing options, and get an estimate for their project.

What We Do

We print custom 3D models for a variety of projects. Whether you need a small replacement part, a prototype, a display piece, or a custom design, Made to Layer helps turn your digital model into a physical object.

Our goal is to make the 3D printing process easy to understand. Instead of worrying about complicated printing settings, customers can select the options they need and get an instant quote before placing an order.
